LICENSE NOTES
- Orphadata Science files are available under CC BY 4.0 according to Orphadata.
- WHO materials have their own reuse terms; verify the applicable license for each asset.
- Do not copy proprietary clinical guideline text into the dataset. Store source metadata and
  concise, original structured summaries instead.
- SNOMED CT has separate licensing requirements; do not redistribute SNOMED content unless
  your licensing permits it.

WHY TREATMENT IS SEPARATE
Orphanet's free science datasets provide nomenclature, mappings, classifications, phenotypes,
genes, epidemiology and natural history. Detailed disease abstracts/treatment resources may
be part of other Orphanet products or external clinical guidelines. Treatment should therefore
be populated from current evidence/guidelines with publication date and verification status,
rather than guessed from disease names.
