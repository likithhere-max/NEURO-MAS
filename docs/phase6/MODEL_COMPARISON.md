# Phase 6: Model Comparison

**Status: `NOT_RUN`.** No comparison result exists, because the data needed to produce held-out predictions are unavailable
(`DATA_AUTHENTICATION.md`, D1). This document is the pre-registered comparison plan plus the design-stage planning figures.
Every results table is therefore empty by design; nothing here may be cited as a performance finding. No clinical validity is claimed.

## 1. What will be compared (all frozen before any result: protocol `p6-1`, SHA-256 `7fd315bb...606ec8`)

Registered baselines (protocol `p4a-1`, unchanged): the candidate pools and nested subject-grouped cross-validation as registered.
Prespecified extension (`--candidate-set p6-1-ext`), each with a written hypothesis (`HYPOTHESES` in `ml/validation/candidates_p6.py`):

| Family | Hypothesis (written before any result) | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| `LR_unweighted_C*` | without re-weighting, scores are closer to probabilities; calibration improves with similar ranking | both |
| `LR_enet_C*` | elastic net helps with many correlated voice features | both |
| `LR_spline_C*` | within-patient trend in time/baseline is non-linear | risk |
| `HGB_regularised` | heavy regularisation captures interactions without small-HGB variance | both |
| `HGB_monotone_time` | worsening should not become less likely as time grows (monotone constraint) | risk sets with `test_time` |

A kernel SVM was considered and excluded before any run (deprecated probability output; the calibrated replacement would calibrate on ungrouped rows).
Hyperparameter choice happens in the **inner** loop on outer-training subjects only. A family that does not beat its comparator is reported as not better.

## 2. Design (fixed)

* Outer evaluation: subject-grouped, repeated over five seeds (base 20261023); inner selection: `StratifiedGroupKFold(3)` inside outer-training rows.
* Threshold fixed at 0.5 a priori; no operating point is chosen from evaluation data.
* Uncertainty: cluster (subject) bootstrap, percentile 95%, **the same resamples for every metric and model** so differences are paired.
* Extension vs registered comparisons are made only if every `(seed, row)` has the same outer fold in both runs; otherwise the report refuses the comparison (tested).
* Seeds are split-sensitivity checks, not independent replications; effective units are about 42 patients (risk) and 8 healthy subjects (disease).

## 3. Metrics reported per model (`ml/evalkit/clinical_metrics.py`, verified against scikit-learn and hand calculations in tests)

AUROC, PR-AUC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, balanced accuracy, accuracy, confusion matrix with denominators, Brier score,
log loss, calibration intercept and slope, a 5-bin reliability table (a table, not a plot: no calibration plots are generated), net-benefit decision curves (diagnostic only), subgroup results above
minimum sizes (>= 8 clusters, >= 20 rows per class), and paired differences with subject-bootstrap intervals.

## 4. Voice-value study (workstream 5)

Identical subject-level splits for all four arms:

| Arm | Feature set | Question |
|---|---|---|
| A | `TIME_ONLY` | what time alone achieves |
| B | `TIME_BASELINE` (and `TIME_BASELINE_DEMOGRAPHIC`) | what time plus baseline severity achieve |
| C | `FULL` | does voice add anything beyond B |
| D | `VOICE_ONLY` | is voice alone informative |

Decision rule, unchanged from `p4a-1`: voice adds measurable information only if the paired AUC interval of `FULL` minus **each**
of `TIME_BASELINE` and `TIME_BASELINE_DEMOGRAPHIC` excludes 0 for **every** seed. Otherwise the result is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not "voice has no value".

### Planning estimate of the data needed (`DESIGN_STAGE_PLANNING_NOT_EVIDENCE`)

`backend/ml/validation/results/phase6/voice_sample_size_planning.json`, simulated generator with an arbitrary signal unit, 12 repetitions per cell
(about +/-28 points of Monte-Carlo error on each rate), single reference (`TIME_BASELINE`) and not the full intersection rule, so the real power is lower:

| Mean true AUC gain of FULL over TIME_BASELINE | 42 patients | 84 patients | 168 patients |
|---|---|---|---|
| about +0.03 | detected 33% of runs | 58% | 100% |
| about +0.08 to +0.09 | 83% | 100% | 100% |

Reading: a voice gain near +0.03 AUC is probably **not** resolvable with the 42 patients available and may need about 84-168 patients;
a gain near +0.09 would likely be. The simulated voice signal is independent across sessions within a patient, real voice is autocorrelated,
and extrapolation outside the simulated cells is not supported. These are not the study's results.
